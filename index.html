Stephen Biernacki

Stephen Biernacki is a conceptual artist in Pennsylvania. He has spent more than two decades in marketing, the business of being seen. His art faces the other way, toward what goes unwatched and unexplained. Many of his pieces are mechanisms built around feelings, written in code and running on real time, specific dates, sound, attention, or participation. Some reveal more to whoever gives more, and some keep their answers to themselves.
