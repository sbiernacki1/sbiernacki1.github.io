Syntax/Synapse

In Syntax/Synapse, AI reinterprets phrases whose meanings transcend their literal expression. Drawing aesthetically from AI variants of a physical collage and three color palettes, this collection visualizes language’s abstract concepts by fusing cryptic sayings with AI’s unpredictable nature. Artwork titles are the exact prompts used.
